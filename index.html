The ritual

Light it before the first song.

  1. 01Trim the wick to a quarter inch before every burn.
  2. 02Let it pool. The first burn should melt all the way to the glass, about two hours.
  3. 03Play the record the candle is named for. That's the whole point.
  4. 04Lid on, flame out. Keep the glass for whatever comes next.
